It is not just a resume folder. It is the source of truth for:
- Master resume
- Tailored resumes
- LinkedIn updates
- Promotion packets
- Annual reviews
- Recruiter summaries
- Interview prep
- Proposal bios
- Business-development positioning

## Core Narrative

Technical leader who bridges software engineering, customer communication, and business value.

Nathan's differentiator is not simply that he writes code. It is that he can take ambiguous technical and business problems, work directly with customers, coordinate across teams, evaluate technology decisions through business value and ROI, and deliver practical software systems.

## Repository Rules

- Never delete accomplishments from the inventory.
- Optimize for finding information, not eliminating duplication.
- Tailored resumes are outputs, not the source of truth.
- Add wins, praise, metrics, and leadership examples as soon as they happen.
- Review quarterly.

## Source Hierarchy

Gold: organization files and raw facts  
Silver: inventory summaries and narratives  
Bronze: resumes and application-specific outputs
