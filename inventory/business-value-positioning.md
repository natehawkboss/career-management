# Business Value Positioning

Nathan's finance degree and master's in software management create a differentiated story: he understands how management evaluates technology decisions.

## Core Angle

Technology decisions are not only technical. They are management decisions involving value, ROI, risk, opportunity cost, staffing, operational impact, maintainability, and customer value.

## Resume Themes

- Translate business needs into software requirements.
- Evaluate technical decisions through ROI and long-term maintainability.
- Understand how executives and managers think about technology investment.
- Communicate technical tradeoffs to non-technical stakeholders.
- Bridge customers, product needs, engineering teams, and management priorities.

## Suggested Resume Language

- Combine engineering depth with finance and software management training to evaluate technical decisions through business value, ROI, risk, and maintainability.
- Partner with customers and stakeholders to translate business objectives into technical requirements and product direction.
- Bring a management-oriented perspective to architecture, delivery planning, and technology investment decisions.

## Interview Talking Point

"My finance background and software management master's help me think about software the way management thinks about it: not just what is technically possible, but what creates value, what reduces risk, what is maintainable, and what is worth the investment."
