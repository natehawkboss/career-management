# Professional Philosophy

- Technology decisions should be evaluated through business value, ROI, risk, maintainability, and delivery speed.
- Good engineers do not just ask, "Can we build this?" They ask, "Should we build this, and what value does it create?"
- Customer relationships are a technical advantage because better communication produces better requirements and better systems.
- Architecture should balance maintainability, delivery speed, cost, and organizational constraints.
- AI should augment engineering judgment, accelerate delivery, and automate low-value work without replacing critical review.
- Cross-functional communication is a Staff-level engineering skill.
- The best technical leaders can explain tradeoffs to executives, customers, scientists, product teams, and engineers.
